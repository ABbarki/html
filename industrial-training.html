Service Details

Practical training built around your plant

Since 2010, General Automation-dz has trained operators, technicians and engineers in Algeria. Courses are held in our training centre in Sétif or at your site, and we build custom modules to match your requirements.

Topics include DCS, SIS, control valves, instrumentation, safety relief valves, buses and communication technologies, and fire & gas systems. Our trainers have delivered courses for organisations such as Sonatrach and Sonelgaz.
