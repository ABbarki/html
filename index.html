Which service do I actually need?

Book the inspection first and we will tell you. If the decking is sound we quote a repair; if the wear is widespread we quote a replacement. The survey is free either way, so nothing hinges on the answer.

Do you fit gutters on their own?

Yes. Gutters, fascias and downpipes are a standalone job for us, not an add-on we only do alongside a roof. We size them to your roof area so heavy rain drains away from the foundations properly.

How often should a roof be inspected?

Twice a year is the honest answer: once after winter and once before it. If your roof is over fifteen years old or has taken a storm, add a check after any heavy weather that moves debris around.

What roofing materials do you install?

Architectural asphalt shingles on most homes, with standing seam metal, cedar and synthetic slate available. We show you samples and the lifespan of each at quoting, then fit whichever you pick.

Do you cover emergency repairs after a storm?
